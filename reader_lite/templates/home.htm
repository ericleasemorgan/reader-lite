Reader Lite: A Tool for Interactive Reading

Reader Lite is a tool to supplement the reading of four pre-selected books:

  1. Emma by Jane Austen
  2. The Iliad and the Odyssey by Homer
  3. Moby Dick by Herman Melville
  4. Walden Pond by Henry David Thoreau

Through the use of this tool your understanding of these four books will be enhanced.

For example, the very first sentence of Moby Dick is "Call me Ishmael." Well, the active reader (you) will automatically ask, "Who is Ishmael and why should I care?" Using this tool you can ask and answer this question quickly and easily.

Think of Reader Lite as if it were a traditional back-of-the-book index but made better with a set of super powers.

Reader Lite tool was written by me, Eric Lease Morgan <eric_morgan@infomotions.com>. As a librarian I have always been fascinated with the collection, organization, preservation, and dissemination of data, information, and knowledge. Reader Lite is simply another application in that vein.
